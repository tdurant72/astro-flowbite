# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Marketing and communications teams for non-profit organizations. They are looking for expertise on how to navigate the shift to AI search and improve their organization's visibility.

## Product Purpose
To help non-profits get found by AI search engines through expert guidance, technical implementation, and strategic content. Success looks like non-profit clients appearing as primary sources in AI-generated answers.

## Brand Personality
Helpful, Knowledgeable, Bold. The tone should be authoritative yet accessible, focusing on providing real value rather than generic "AI hype."

## Anti-references
- **AI Slop**: Generic gradients, overuse of purple/neon glows, and "floating brain" or "digital matrix" imagery.
- **SaaS Clichés**: Hero-metric templates, identical card grids, and generic illustration styles.
- **Nested Complexity**: Overly complex navigation or information architecture that obscures the core message.

## Design Principles
- **Machine-Readable Authority**: Prioritize semantic HTML and structured data to ensure the design supports the core mission of AI search optimization.
- **Intentional Boldness**: Use high contrast and purposeful typography to convey confidence and knowledge without relying on decorative gimmicks.
- **Helpful Precision**: Every design element must serve a purpose. Avoid "filler" UI; if an element doesn't help the user or the machine understand the message, remove it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance as a baseline.
- Focus on clear typography and high-contrast ratios to ensure information is accessible to a wide range of non-profit stakeholders.
